Privacy Policy for myClipClip

Effective Date: November 21, 2026  •  Publisher: Kijo Kim

1. Overview

myClipClip is a local-first Windows clipboard manager, snippet expander, and AI search utility. We respect your privacy and do not collect, transmit, sell, or store any of your personal data, clipboard history, or snippets on publisher-controlled servers.

2. Local Data Storage

  • Clipboard History & Snippets: All captured text, file paths, images, snippets, and app preferences are stored exclusively on your local device inside %APPDATA%\myClipClip\.
  • Retention: Unpinned clipboard history is automatically pruned according to your configured retention setting (10 days by default), and you can clear history at any time in Settings.
  • API Keys: Any third-party AI API keys you enter are encrypted locally at rest using Windows Data Protection API (DPAPI) and are never shared with the publisher.

3. Optional Third-Party AI Requests (Bring Your Own Key)

  • myClipClip never sends clipboard content over the network automatically.
  • Only when you explicitly trigger AI Search and submit a prompt does the app send the selected copied content and prompt directly from your device to the AI provider you selected and configured (such as OpenRouter, Google Gemini, DeepSeek, Anthropic Claude, OpenAI, or a custom local/remote OpenAI-compatible endpoint).
  • Data sent to a third-party AI provider is governed by that provider's own privacy policy.

4. Telemetry & Analytics

myClipClip contains no analytics SDKs, advertising trackers, or background telemetry.

5. Contact

For privacy questions or support, please contact Kijo Kim via the support contact listed on the Microsoft Store product page.
